How to Manage Electronic Waste?

  • Design for recyclability and sustainability
  • Implement Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)
  • Develop closed-loop recycling systems
  • Encourage repair, refurbishment, and reuse
  • Strengthen regulations and enforcement
  • Educate consumers about e-waste impacts
  • Invest in e-waste management infrastructure

Benefits of Proper E-Waste Management

  • Economic Benefits
  • Reduces environmental pollution
  • Protects human health
  • Reduces soil pollution
  • Reduces air pollution
